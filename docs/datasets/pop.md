## Overview

Annual population estimates for Bolivia's 339 municipalities, 2001–2020: gridded population counts summed over each municipality. They are the denominators for per-capita indicators such as night-time lights per capita.

- **Dataset id:** `pop`
- **Categories:** Social

## Scale: municipality

- **Units:** 339 of 339 municipality units
- **Period:** 2001–2020, annual (6,780 rows: one per unit and year)
- **Source geometry:** DS4Bolivia polygons (bolivia339geoqueryFull, keyed by asdf_id), matched one-to-one to the GADM 4.1 municipalities by area overlap

## Notes

- The counts were computed by DS4Bolivia on its own municipal polygons, which differ slightly from the GADM boundaries used for geo IDs; `asdf_id` keeps the DS4Bolivia polygon ID.
- DS4Bolivia's population README does not name the gridded product; its Earth Engine workflow (apps/geoexplorer1v100bolivia.js, code/aggregate-satellite-embedings-to-adm-pop-weighted1.js) uses WorldPop 100 m, which covers 2000–2020.
- These are gridded estimates, not the official INE projections. The SDG dataset carries the Atlas population for 2020 (`sdg.population_2020`), which differs.
