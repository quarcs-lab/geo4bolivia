## Overview

The SDG 15 (Life on land) index of the Municipal Atlas of the Sustainable Development Goals in Bolivia 2020 (SDSN Bolivia) and the 2 indicators behind it, for Bolivia's 339 municipalities, with the Atlas population and urbanization rate as context. Each variable is stored at its own reference year, so the panel is sparse: a variable has values only in its year. The index is stored at 2019, the latest reference year of its indicators.

- **Dataset id:** `sdg15`
- **Categories:** Environment

## Scale: municipality

- **Units:** 339 of 339 municipality units
- **Period:** 2012–2020, annual (1,356 rows: one per unit and year)
- **Source geometry:** Municipal statistics of the Atlas, linked by INE municipality code

## Notes

- Reference years come from the variable labels. Indicators averaged over a period are stored at its midpoint, rounded down (e.g. average 2014–17 at 2015); the period is given in the description and `geo:year` holds the year used.
- The SDG 15 index (`index_sdg15`) summarises indicators from different years; it is stored at 2019, the latest of them.
- Load one indicator with its year, e.g. `geo.load("sdg15", variables="index_sdg15", years=2019)`, or every variable as a cross-section with `geo.load("sdg15").groupby("geo_id").first()`.
- Most indicators are rates, shares or indices, so their aggregation rule is `none`. Population-weighted averages can be built with the `pop` dataset, which covers every reference year: `geo.aggregate(df, to="department", rules={"index_sdg15": "weighted_mean:pop"}, weights=geo.load("pop"))`.
- In DS4Bolivia the rows of three homonym pairs (San Javier in Beni and Santa Cruz, El Puente in Tarija and Santa Cruz, Santa Rosa in Beni and Pando) are attached to the other polygon of the pair. Here the indicators are linked by INE code (`mun_id`), so each lies in its correct municipality; see raw/README.md.
- `population_2020` is the Atlas (INE-based) population and differs from the gridded `pop` dataset.
