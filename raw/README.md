## DS4Bolivia's swapped homonyms

Three pairs of municipalities share a name across departments: San Javier (Beni, Santa Cruz), El Puente (Tarija, Santa Cruz) and Santa Rosa (Beni, Pando). In DS4Bolivia, the attribute rows of each pair (name, department, INE code, and the SDG Atlas variables joined by code) are attached to the other polygon of the pair, while the data computed from the polygons (population, night-time lights, satellite embeddings) belong to the polygon. The evidence:

- the polygon of `asdf_id` 57 lies in Beni, but its row says San Javier, Santa Cruz (`mun_id` 71102), with an Atlas population of 16,027, the size of San Javier in Santa Cruz (its WorldPop population is 8,824)
- after swapping the rows back, every INE department and province code matches exactly one GADM department and province; before, six municipalities broke that nesting

`lookups/municipalities339_gadm.csv` records both keys (`swapped = True` for the six), and each builder joins on the key its data follow.
