# Image prompts for the documentation

Generate them with `uv run python tools/build_images.py --generate` (Codex CLI), or run each prompt in ChatGPT
and save the picture in docs/images/originals/ with the file name in its heading; then
`uv run python tools/build_images.py`.

### g4b-hero.png
2:1 wide banner. Cinematic, editorial, luminous. Palette: deep teal-black (#071417, #0b1e22), glowing turquoise (#5ad6c6), flamingo pink and rose (#ff8597, #d1405a), soft salt-white and a little warm sand (#e7c27d). Strict rules: Bolivian landscape only; abstract map-like shapes are allowed, but NO country outline, NO borders or boundary lines, NO globe, NO other countries or continents; no people's faces; no text, letters, numbers, axis labels, legends, logos, flags or watermarks. Bolivia's night-time lights seen from orbit: a high view of the Earth's surface at night over the Andes, the Altiplano and the eastern lowlands, with no coastline, no borders and no visible country shape. City lights form clusters of warm gold and rose: one large dense cluster on the high plateau beside a dark lake, others in the valleys and a large one on the eastern plain, villages as faint specks, the dark snow-capped cordillera between them. A faint turquoise hexagon grid lies over part of the land, aggregating the lights into cells. In the foreground, in orbit, a detailed satellite with solar panels senses the lights below with a soft turquoise sensor glow. A slim translucent panel of three tiny unlabelled charts floats in space at right: a scatter plot with a fitted line, a Lorenz curve, a trend line. Logic of the picture: the satellite only observes the land (it images the terrain or senses its lights); every analytical graphic (points, network lines, cells, charts) is a translucent data layer or overlay derived from the land, never an object lying on the ground. Charts are tiny and unlabelled: no axis labels, no numbers, no letters. Leave the upper-left corner of the picture as calm, empty dark sky or space, with nothing in it, for an emblem added later. Elegant, modern, uncluttered.

The SDG colour wheel is not in the prompt: `tools/build_images.py` draws it on the picture (`HERO_SDG_WHEEL`).
