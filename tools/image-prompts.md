# Image prompts for the documentation

Generate them with `uv run python tools/build_images.py --generate` (Codex CLI), or run each prompt in ChatGPT
and save the picture in docs/images/originals/ with the file name in its heading; then
`uv run python tools/build_images.py`.

Palette: deep teal-black (#071417, #0b1e22), glowing turquoise (#5ad6c6), flamingo pink and rose (#ff8597, #d1405a), soft salt-white and a little warm sand (#e7c27d). Spatial data science over Bolivia's landscapes, at blue hour, from a high oblique aerial angle. Foreground: the Salar de Uyuni, its hexagonal salt polygons lit like a LISA cluster map: one connected group of cells glowing rose (a hot spot), another group glowing turquoise (a cold spot), a few single cells of the opposite colour among them (outliers), the rest soft salt-white. Middle distance: the lights of Altiplano towns as glowing nodes, each marked by a small luminous location pin, joined to their nearest neighbours by thin straight turquoise lines, like a spatial weights network. A faint latitude-longitude grid of thin light lines curves over the land. On the horizon the snow peaks of the Cordillera Real and the dark green lowlands. In the starry sky, faint translucent holographic charts float without any labels or numbers: a scatter plot of dots with a fitted straight line, a Lorenz curve under its diagonal, a declining line chart, and a small abstract mosaic of shaded square tiles like a choropleth; a small satellite casts a soft scanning beam onto the plain. Keep it elegant and readable, the left third calmer than the right, not cluttered.
